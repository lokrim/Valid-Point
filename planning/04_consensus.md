# Cross-agent geometry and consensus disposition — 2026-10-08

Receiver geometry may be useful independent information, but consensus is not a required factor. S05 peer voting is superseded and deferred. The archive has three vehicles plus one RSU principal; top and dome are two sensors under that same principal. Agreement does not establish truth, common visibility or independence of localization errors.

R02 may make a bounded **diagnostic** comparison: transform a tested sender and the latest already available top receiver cloud to a common frame, retain exact skew and pose ages, and display overlap maps and directed geometric distances on a frozen potential-overlap crop. Absence of nearby receiver points is not proof of a fabricated sender point; occlusion and unequal sampling can cause it. No actual visibility mask is available from the verified release. `gt_visibility` generated from labeled point support is prohibited operationally.

To promote X into scoring, record a dated pre-comparison amendment specifying independently justified comparable support, raw distance units, dynamic-object/timing confounds, clean per-sensor/skew reference fit, unknown regions, support requirements, runtime bounds, and separately calibrated X-only/max ablations. Exclude the tested sender from any peer reference. Without support, leave X diagnostic/unknown. Do not convert missing overlap into a penalty or drop hard scenes after exposure.

Broader peer quorum, learned peer selection, two colluders, Sybil identities and majority rules need a separate research question and assumptions. Deferring them removes the old S05 prerequisite for real intake and overlays; it does not establish their safety or usefulness.
