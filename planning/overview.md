# Researcher and professor overview

We will ask whether simple, explainable checks can reveal specified alterations to one participant's LiDAR observations while leaving ordinary observations relatively conforming. We will replay a real recording of several participants, preserve an untouched copy, and create controlled altered copies. This tests changes to recorded point clouds; it does not physically simulate laser hardware or traffic responding to an attack.

The next step is small: inspect one approximately 30-second development archive. Confirm what its point files and vehicle pose tables actually contain, check how the different views line up, and plot a short multi-agent sequence in metres and seconds. No comparison or final holdout result is needed to make this decision.

The existing project provides useful geometry, causal-input, scoring and reproducibility examples. Its reported detection and false-alarm numbers come from constructed cases. They are not Mixed Signals performance. Its motion residual compares pose changes with reported velocity and cannot by itself tell whether points were removed or moved. Usable measured velocity is not established by the public reader.

Start with sensor-specific density evidence; test motion-compensated temporal geometry where the data supports it. Keep the instantaneous measurements and a small time-dependent score separate. Plot the selected agent and honest companions before, during and after each alteration. Some removal, plausible additions and coordinated changes may be indistinguishable from occlusion, moving objects or sensor faults. Those limitations are part of the result.

Fit normal ranges on clean training segments and choose alarm thresholds on separate clean segments processed through the real pipeline. Evaluate at frozen settings, show every attempted intervention and abstention, and report scene-level uncertainty. The outcome may be a useful baseline, a constrained demonstration, or an informative negative study. Novelty and publication suitability require a later literature and evidence assessment.

Only immediate researcher decision: authorize the mini_7 download and storage budget described in [development intake](development_intake.md). Algorithm choice is resolved through documented feasibility criteria, not a preference poll.
