# Bandwidth and downstream tasks: deferred — 2026-10-08

The primary deliverable is causal real-cloud replay evidence and score trajectories. Bandwidth allocation, next-frame quotas, transmission-saving claims, payload selection and task-quality proxies are deferred; no primary stage depends on them. This replaces the earlier H003/H009 quota objective and old S06/S08/S09 policy requirements. The complete earlier specification remains in the dated planning snapshot for historical review, not automatic execution.

Replay cloud overlays do not establish transmission savings. No detector, AP, fusion gain, learned combiner, hindsight policy or probability-of-honesty claim is part of R01–R07.

If later invoked, define a new task with trusted enforcement location, exact serialization and overhead, attempted/transmitted/admitted/processed/fused counters, causal future-only allocation, censored observation handling, honest-view effects and equal actual-byte comparisons.
